# Ray index: field missions, 2026-11-22

A field mission spent 97% of its tick in one question: what does this ray meet
first (`SharedVisibility::first_hit`). `ObstacleLayer::query` answered it with
every obstacle in the ray's bounding box, collected through a `BTreeSet`, and
each one's footprint was then intersected exactly. The pod LiDAR's rays were
59% of the run and the navigation camera's 39%.

`ObstacleLayer::first_hard_hit` walks a bounding-volume tree over the hard
obstacles' 3D boxes (`rust/argusnet-world/src/ray_index.rs`), nearest first,
cut off at the nearest hit so far. The exact test and its answer are
unchanged. `query` now sorts a list in place of filling a set.

## Cost

West Pleasant View (592 buildings on the map), `--profile field_v2`, seed 7,
600 s, 2,401 ticks, one worker, release build. Apple M5 Pro with other work
running (load average about 9), so read the instructions before the seconds:
they barely move between runs, while the same build took 10–14 s at a load
average above 12. The base is `71919d7`. The wall and tick times were taken just before
the last rule under "What can change" was added; the instruction count was
retaken after it and did not move.

## What did not change

Replays are byte-identical to the base:

- 14 of 14 canonical replays, and every lifecycle check equal;
- 14 field runs on West Pleasant View: `field_v1` and `field_v2` at seeds 7,
  42, 707, 1010, 4451, 9002 and 13127;
- 6 no-prior runs there (the range scanner, with `--nav-camera`, and the rig
  with `--lidar-returns` and a sweep; seeds 7 and 42) and 2 prior runs.

## What can change

The index refuses a hit that is not on the solid it names. A ray that misses a
corner by less than `point_on_segment`'s 1 µm tolerance, with its midpoint at
that corner, crosses no edge yet counts as inside the footprint for its whole
length; the exact test then reports a solid where there is none (in the test,
7 m past its end). That fault is in `segment_polygon_intervals` and still
reaches line of sight, which does not use the index. No replay here met it.

## Tests

`ray_index::tests` (6): against testing every obstacle, bit for bit, over
random towns with roofs, cylinders, concave footprints and vegetation; along
walls and through corners; ties; and a bound on the number of exact tests, so
a return to testing everything fails without a clock. Seven mutants of the
index each fail at least one.
